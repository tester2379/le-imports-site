Asset note
==========

Brand logos (assets/logos/) and product photography (assets/products/)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Supplied by L&E Imports: 15 brand logos and 60 product images, taken from the
reference documents listed below. Every product's name, the brand it belongs to
and the image that goes with it come from those documents; nothing is inferred.

One exception worth knowing about: the supplied MIKE AND IKE logo was a
stock-site file with the transparency checkerboard (and that site's watermark)
baked into the pixels, so it rendered as a grey grid on a white card. Only that
background has been made transparent - the artwork itself is untouched. It is
still worth asking L&E for the brand's proper logo file.

Categories are the one judgement call on this site: they are assigned per BRAND
from the category list in the plan, because the documents do not carry a
category per product.

Site photography (hero and section images)
------------------------------------------
Unsplash prototype imagery; replace with L&E Imports-approved company
photography before production.

Typography
----------
Inter and Montserrat are loaded from Google Fonts ONLY after the visitor accepts
the notice (see cookie-notice.js and the data-consent-href link in each page).
The site is designed to read correctly on the system fallback if they refuse, so
never turn that link back into a plain stylesheet href or a CSS @import.
