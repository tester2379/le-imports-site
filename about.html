ABOUT L&E IMPORTS

Connecting brands
with businesses.

L&E Imports is a Malta-based importer, wholesale supplier and distributor of food products for business customers. We work with retailers, supermarkets, convenience shops, restaurants, cafés, hotels and catering operators.

Our role is to make quality imported products available to the businesses that bring them to customers every day — with a professional, approachable service built around clear communication.
