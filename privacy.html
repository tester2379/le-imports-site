Privacy Policy

L&E Imports · last updated 20 September 2026

This explains what we do with your personal data when you use this website, and the rights you have over it. We have tried to write it plainly rather than in legal language.

Who is responsible for your data

L&E Imports is the data controller — we decide what is collected and why. You can reach us about anything on this page at:

Email: info@leimports.com
Phone: 9947 4578

This website is built and hosted for us by DSM Communications Malta, who act on our instructions as a data processor and may not use your data for their own purposes.

What we collect

This site is a catalogue. It has no contact form, no account and no newsletter. We collect no personal data through this website — there is no form, no account and no newsletter sign-up. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we hold whatever you tell us there so we can reply.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no tracking cookies. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel and no profiling of visitors. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so nothing about your visit is sent to another company.

Why we are allowed to use it

For replying to an enquiry you have made, our legal basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in answering people who contact us, or steps before a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) where you are asking about buying something.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. It is seen only by us, by DSM Communications as our processor, and where you contact us on WhatsApp, by WhatsApp Ireland Ltd as part of carrying that message.

How long we keep it

We keep the messages you send us only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless we must keep records for tax or warranty reasons.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit what we do with it;
  • hand it to you, or another provider, in a portable form;
  • stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where you gave it. Withdrawing does not undo what we did beforehand.

Write to us using the details above and we will answer within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with our answer

You can complain to the Maltese supervisory authority, the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC), Floor 2, Airways House, High Street, Sliema SLM 1549 — idpc.org.mt. You can complain to them without coming to us first.

Changes

If we change how we use your data we will update this page and change the date at the top.
